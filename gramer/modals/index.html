B1 · Orta

Must / Have to / Should konu anlatımı

Zorunluluk ve tavsiye · Kipler

Bu kip fiilleri zorunluluk, gereklilik ve tavsiye anlatır. "must" konuşanın kendi hissettiği güçlü zorunluluk ya da kurallar, "have to" dış koşullardan gelen zorunluluk, "should" ise tavsiye için kullanılır.

Özet tablo

KipAnlamıÖrnek
must-meli (güçlü, kişisel/kural)I must call my mum.
mustn'tyasakYou mustn't smoke here.
have tozorunda olmak (dış etken)I have to wear a uniform.
don't have togerek yokYou don't have to come.
should-meli (tavsiye)You should see a doctor.

Örnek cümleler

Drivers must stop at a red light.

Sürücüler kırmızı ışıkta durmalı.

I have to get up early on weekdays.

Hafta içi erken kalkmak zorundayım.

You don't have to pay. It's free.

Ödemek zorunda değilsin. Ücretsiz.

You should drink more water.

Daha fazla su içmelisin.

Sık yapılan hatalar

You mustn't come if you're busy. → You don't have to come if you're busy."mustn't" yasak demektir; gereksizlik için "don't have to".
She musts go. → She must go.Kip fiiller -s almaz.
You should to rest. → You should rest."should"dan sonra "to" gelmez.

Kendini dene

Bu konudan 5 örnek soru. Cevabı görmek için soruya dokun; tüm alıştırmalar ve bölüm testi uygulamada.

1. You ___ use your phone during the exam. It's forbidden. (don't have to / mustn't / shouldn't to / haven't)

Cevap: mustn't — Yasak: mustn't.

2. Tomorrow is Sunday, so I ___ go to work. (mustn't / don't have to / shouldn't / can't)

Cevap: don't have to — Gerek yok: don't have to.

3. You look tired. You ___ go to bed early. (should / must to / have / mustn't)

Cevap: should — Tavsiye: should.

4. In Turkey, people ___ drive on the right. (should / have to / can / mustn't)

Cevap: have to — Dış kural/zorunluluk: have to.

5. She ___ wear glasses to read. (has to / have to / must to / should to)

Cevap: has to — "she" ile: has to.
