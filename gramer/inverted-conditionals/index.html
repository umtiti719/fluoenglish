Inverted Conditionals konu anlatımı

Had I known… / Should you need… / Were I to… · If'siz koşul cümleleri

Resmî İngilizcede koşul cümlelerinde "if" atılıp yardımcı fiil başa alınabilir. "If I had known" yerine "Had I known", "If you should need" yerine "Should you need" denir. Anlam aynıdır ama cümle daha resmî ve akıcı olur. İş e-postalarında, resmî yazılarda ve edebî metinlerde çok kullanılır.

Ne zaman kullanılır?

  • Üçüncü tip (geçmiş): Had I known about the problem, I would have helped.Sorunu bilseydim yardım ederdim.
  • İkinci tip (varsayım): Were I in your position, I would accept the offer.Senin yerinde olsam teklifi kabul ederdim.
  • Birinci tip (olasılık, kibar): Should you have any questions, please contact us.Herhangi bir sorunuz olursa lütfen bize ulaşın.
  • Gelecekteki varsayım: Were the company to close, hundreds would lose their jobs.Şirket kapanacak olsa yüzlerce kişi işini kaybeder.

Cümle yapısı

HadHad + özne + V3, … would have + V3Had she left earlier, she would have caught the train.Daha erken çıksaydı trene yetişirdi.
WereWere + özne + (to V1), … would + V1Were I rich, I would buy an island.Zengin olsam bir ada alırdım.
ShouldShould + özne + V1, … will/imperativeShould you need help, call me.Yardıma ihtiyacın olursa beni ara.

Örnek cümleler

Had we known about the traffic, we would have taken the train.

Trafiği bilseydik trene binerdik.

Should you require further information, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Daha fazla bilgiye ihtiyacınız olursa lütfen bizimle iletişime geçmekten çekinmeyin.

Were I to win the lottery, I would quit my job.

Piyangoyu kazanacak olsam işimi bırakırdım.

Had it not been for your help, I would have failed.

Senin yardımın olmasaydı başarısız olurdum.

Were it not for the rain, we could go out.

Yağmur olmasa dışarı çıkabilirdik.

Should the flight be delayed, we will inform you by text.

Uçuş rötar yaparsa size mesajla bilgi vereceğiz.

Sık yapılan hatalar

Had I knew, I would have come. → Had I known, I would have come."Had"den sonra fiilin 3. hâli gelir.
If had I known, I would have told you. → Had I known, I would have told you.Devrik yapıda "if" kullanılmaz.
Hadn't I seen it, I wouldn't believe it. → Had I not seen it, I wouldn't believe it.Olumsuz devrik koşulda "not" özneden sonra gelir; kısaltma yapılmaz.

Kendini dene

Bu konudan 5 örnek soru. Cevabı görmek için soruya dokun; tüm alıştırmalar ve bölüm testi uygulamada.

1. ___ I known you were ill, I would have visited you. (If / Had / Were / Should)

Cevap: Had — Geçmiş: Had + özne + V3.

2. ___ you need any help, just let me know. (Should / Had / Were / Would)

Cevap: Should — Olası gelecek: Should you need…

3. ___ I in your shoes, I would apologise. (Was / Were / Had / Should)

Cevap: Were — Varsayım: Were I…

4. Had it not ___ for the doctor, he would have died. (be / been / being / was)

Cevap: been — Had it not been for…

5. ___ it not for her support, the project would fail. (Had / Were / Should / Did)

Cevap: Were — Şimdiki varsayım: Were it not for…
