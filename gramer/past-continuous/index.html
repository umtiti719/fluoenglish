Past Continuous konu anlatımı

was / were + -ing · Geçmişte süren olaylar

Past Continuous, geçmişte belirli bir anda devam etmekte olan işleri anlatır. Türkçedeki "-yordu" ekine karşılık gelir. Çoğunlukla Simple Past ile birlikte, süren bir olayın başka bir olayla kesildiğini anlatmak için kullanılır.

Ne zaman kullanılır?

  • Geçmişte belirli bir anda süren iş: At 8 pm I was having dinner.
  • Kesilen olay: I was sleeping when the phone rang.
  • Aynı anda süren iki olay: She was cooking while he was reading.
  • Hikâyelerde arka plan: The sun was shining…

Cümle yapısı

OlumluÖzne + was/were + fiil-ingThey were playing cards.
OlumsuzÖzne + wasn't/weren't + fiil-ingI wasn't listening.
SoruWas/Were + özne + fiil-ing?What were you doing?

Örnek cümleler

I was walking home when it started to rain.

Yağmur başladığında eve yürüyordum.

While she was studying, her brother was playing games.

O ders çalışırken kardeşi oyun oynuyordu.

What were you doing at 10 last night?

Dün gece saat 10'da ne yapıyordun?

Sık yapılan hatalar

When I was arriving, they left. → When I arrived, they were leaving.Kısa, kesen olay Simple Past; süren olay Past Continuous.
They was watching TV. → They were watching TV.you/we/they ile "were".

Kendini dene

Bu konudan 5 örnek soru. Cevabı görmek için soruya dokun; tüm alıştırmalar ve bölüm testi uygulamada.

1. I ___ a shower when the doorbell rang. (took / was taking / am taking / take)

Cevap: was taking — Süren olay kesiliyor: was taking.

2. While we were driving, we ___ an accident. (saw / were seeing / see / have seen)

Cevap: saw — Kısa olay: Simple Past.

3. What ___ you doing at midnight? (was / did / were / are)

Cevap: were — "you" ile: were.

4. She ___ attention, so she missed the instructions. (didn't pay / wasn't paying / doesn't pay / isn't paying)

Cevap: wasn't paying — Süren durum: wasn't paying.

5. At 9 am yesterday, they ___ breakfast. (had / were having / have / are having)

Cevap: were having — Belirli geçmiş anda süren iş.
