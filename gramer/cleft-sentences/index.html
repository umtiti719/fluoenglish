Cleft Sentences konu anlatımı

It is… that / What I need is… · Vurgulu cümleler

Cleft sentence'lar, cümlenin bir kısmını öne çıkarmak için cümleyi ikiye böler. Türkçede vurguyu kelime sırasıyla ya da tonlamayla yaparız; İngilizcede ise "It was … that" ya da "What … is" kalıplarıyla yaparız.

Ne zaman kullanılır?

  • Kişiyi/şeyi vurgulamak: It was Ali who broke the window.
  • İhtiyacı vurgulamak: What I need is a long holiday.
  • Eylemi vurgulamak: What she did was call the police.
  • Zamanı/yeri vurgulamak: It was in 2015 that we met.

Cümle yapısı

It-cleftIt + be + vurgulanan + that/who + cümlenin geri kalanıIt was my sister who called.
What-cleftWhat + Özne + fiil + be + vurgulananWhat I love is your smile.
EylemWhat + Özne + did + was + V1What he did was apologise.

Örnek cümleler

It was my brother who told me the news.

Haberi bana söyleyen kardeşimdi.

What I need is a cup of strong coffee.

İhtiyacım olan şey sert bir kahve.

It's the noise that I can't stand, not the heat.

Dayanamadığım şey sıcak değil, gürültü.

What she did was call the manager.

Yaptığı şey müdürü aramaktı.

It was in Istanbul that they first met.

İlk kez tanıştıkları yer İstanbul'du.

All I want is a quiet weekend.

Tek istediğim sakin bir hafta sonu.

Sık yapılan hatalar

It was John which helped me. → It was John who helped me.Kişi vurgulanınca "who" (ya da "that") kullanılır.
What I need it is rest. → What I need is rest.What-cleft'te fazladan "it" kullanılmaz.
What he did was to apologised. → What he did was apologise."What … did was"tan sonra yalın fiil (ya da to + V1) gelir.

Kendini dene

Bu konudan 5 örnek soru. Cevabı görmek için soruya dokun; tüm alıştırmalar ve bölüm testi uygulamada.

1. ___ was my mother who taught me to cook. (What / It / That / There)

Cevap: It — It-cleft: It was … who.

2. What I really want ___ a new job. (is / it is / are / be)

Cevap: is — What I want + is.

3. It was in 2010 ___ I moved to Ankara. (which / when / that / where)

Cevap: that — Zaman vurgusunda It was … that.

4. What he did ___ call the police. (is / was / did / were)

Cevap: was — Geçmiş eylem: What he did was + V1.

5. Which sentence emphasises "the price"? (The price shocked me. / It was the price that shocked me. / The price was shocking me. / I was shocked by price.)

Cevap: It was the price that shocked me. — It was the price that…
